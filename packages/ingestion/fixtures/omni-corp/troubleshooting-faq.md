# Omni Corp IT Troubleshooting FAQ

Document owner: Service Desk. Last reviewed: 2026-09-20.

Try these steps before opening a ticket. If they do not help, open a ticket and list what you tried.

## My laptop is slow

1. Restart. A laptop that has run for many days without restarting slows down and misses updates.
2. Close heavy apps and browser tabs. Check CPU and memory in Activity Monitor (Mac) or Task Manager (Windows).
3. Check that OneDrive is not syncing a large folder.
4. Free disk space. Keep at least 15 percent free.
5. Check for pending updates in Self-Service or Company Portal.
6. If it is still slow after a restart, open a `Hardware` ticket and include the model and the time slow periods occur.

## I cannot sign in to an application

- "Wrong password" for Omni ID: reset at https://id.omnicorp.example/reset.
- "Account locked": wait 15 minutes. Ten failed attempts lock the account.
- MFA prompt not arriving: open Microsoft Authenticator and check it is on the right account. Use a backup code if needed. See the MFA guide.
- "No access": request access in the catalog. See the Access Requests guide.

## Outlook, Teams or Zoom problems

See the Email and Calendar guide and the Collaboration Tools guide. The two most common fixes are restarting the app and signing out and back in.

## Bluetooth or headset does not work

Forget the device in Bluetooth settings, put the headset back in pairing mode, and pair again. Choose it as input and output in the call app. For Jabra Evolve2, update the firmware in Jabra Direct.

## Printing

See the Printing and Meeting Room AV guide. The usual cause is printing to the wrong queue. Use **OmniCorp-Secure**.

## Wi-Fi or VPN

See the Wi-Fi guide and the VPN Access guide.

## Screen or display

- External monitor blank: reseat the dock cable, try another port, restart the laptop.
- Display flicker: unplug the dock, reboot, and test the laptop alone.
- Two monitors with wrong resolution: set the resolution in Display settings, and use the dock's DisplayPort or USB-C cable, not an HDMI adapter.

## Laptop battery and charging

- Use the supplied charger. Third-party chargers can damage the battery.
- A swollen battery is a safety risk. Stop using the laptop and bring it to the IT desk at once.

## A software update is asking to restart

Restart within 3 days. Save your work first. Patches protect against active threats.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Lock your screen | Win + L | Control + Command + Q |
| Screenshot of an area | Win + Shift + S | Shift + Command + 4 |
| Force quit an app | Ctrl + Shift + Esc, then End task | Option + Command + Esc |
