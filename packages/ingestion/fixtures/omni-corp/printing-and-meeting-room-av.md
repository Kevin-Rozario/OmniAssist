# Omni Corp Printing and Meeting Room AV

Document owner: Collaboration Services and Workplace. Last reviewed: 2026-08-05.

## Printing

Omni Corp uses badge-release printing (PrintAnywhere).

1. Print to the queue **OmniCorp-Secure** from any managed laptop. Nothing prints yet.
2. Walk to any printer on any floor and tap your badge.
3. Choose the jobs to release. Unreleased jobs are deleted after 24 hours.

- Default is black and white, double-sided. Colour is limited to 100 pages per month per person. Request more through a ticket with a manager's approval.
- Home printing is not supported. Do not install personal printer drivers on company laptops.
- Scan to email sends the scan to your own mailbox only.
- Printed Confidential material must be collected at once and shredded in the bins provided. Restricted data must not be printed.

## Printer problems

| Symptom | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Job does not appear at the printer | Confirm you printed to OmniCorp-Secure, and that the job was sent less than 24 hours ago |
| Badge not recognised | Re-register the badge at the printer with your Omni ID once; if it fails, open a `Hardware` ticket |
| Paper jam or toner empty | Message `#it-help` with the printer label shown on the front, for example `AUS-2-04` |

## Meeting rooms

Rooms use either Zoom Rooms (boardrooms and customer-facing rooms) or Microsoft Teams Rooms (all other rooms).

- Book rooms in Outlook. A room is released if nobody checks in within 10 minutes.
- Join with the touch panel by tapping **Join** on the meeting listed. For a one-off call, tap **Meet Now**.
- Share your laptop screen wirelessly with the room code, or connect with the USB-C cable on the table.

## Meeting room problems

- **Display is blank:** check the input source on the panel, then re-plug the USB-C cable. Press the power button for 5 seconds on the room unit only as a last resort.
- **No sound:** confirm the room microphone is not muted on the panel and the volume is up.
- **Panel shows an error or is offline:** message `#it-help` with the room name, or call the Service Desk. Include the room name from the label on the door.

Walk-up AV help is available at the IT desk during desk hours. For executive events, request AV support at least three business days ahead.
