# Omni Corp Onboarding and Offboarding: IT Checklist

Document owner: Service Desk with People Operations. Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

Joiner, mover and leaver changes start in Workday. IT acts automatically from those events, and managers add extra requests through the catalog.

## Joiners

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 10 business days before start | Manager requests extra access and equipment in the catalog under `Onboarding` |
| 5 business days before start | Laptop and accessories ship, to arrive at least 2 business days before day one |
| 3 business days before start | Accounts are created: Omni ID, email, Slack, Teams, 1Password |
| Day one | Welcome email with temporary credentials; laptop setup and MFA enrollment (see Laptop Setup and MFA guides) |
| Week one | Team access is provisioned and a 30-minute IT orientation is offered on Zoom |

A late request does not block the account, but equipment may arrive after the start date.

## Managers: before the new hire starts

1. Submit the onboarding request with job title, location, laptop model for the role and software needs.
2. Confirm the shipping address with the new hire.
3. Check that Workday shows the correct start date. Wrong dates delay accounts.

## Movers

When someone changes team or role, Workday updates the role. Team-specific access is removed after a 14-day overlap, so request new access early. Laptop swaps are not automatic; request hardware if the new role needs a different model.

## Leavers

| Case | Access removal |
| --- | --- |
| Voluntary leaver | Access disabled at the end of the last working day |
| Involuntary leaver | Access disabled immediately, at the time People Operations notifies IT |
| Contractor | Access disabled when the contract ends, or when the 90-day account expires without an extension |

- Laptops and accessories are returned in the prepaid box within 10 business days.
- OneDrive content is kept for 30 days and transferred to the manager on request. The mailbox becomes a shared mailbox for 90 days and is then deleted.
- Slack and Zoom accounts are deactivated, and shared secrets the person knew are rotated.
- Personal data on a company laptop is not recovered. Remove personal files before the last day.

## Checklist for managers when someone leaves

1. Tell People Operations in advance so Workday has the leaving date.
2. Ask for OneDrive transfer and mailbox delegation in the catalog before the last day.
3. Reassign shared mailboxes, distribution lists and application ownership.
