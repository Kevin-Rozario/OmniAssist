# Omni Corp Travel IT Guide

Document owner: Security Operations with Service Desk. Last reviewed: 2026-08-14.

## Before you travel

- Tell your manager and tell Security through the catalog form **Travel notification** at least 10 business days before international trips. Short domestic trips do not need a notification.
- Update your laptop and restart it. Do not defer updates.
- Check that you can sign in with MFA and keep your backup codes in 1Password, not on paper in your bag.
- Carry only the data you need. Files in OneDrive are available from any browser, so leave sensitive files off the laptop.

## High-risk destinations

Security maintains a list of high-risk countries, reviewed twice a year. For these destinations you must use a loaner laptop and, if needed, a loaner phone. Your normal devices stay at home. Check the current list in the Travel notification form. Examples that have appeared on the list include countries under comprehensive sanctions and countries with known state-sponsored surveillance of visitors.

Loaner devices are requested at least 10 business days before departure and are returned and wiped when you come back.

## While travelling

- Use the company VPN whenever you access internal systems on hotel or public Wi-Fi.
- Never use public USB charging ports. Use your own charger and a plug socket, or a USB data blocker.
- Do not leave devices in a hotel room or checked luggage. Use the safe if one exists.
- Be careful of shoulder surfing on planes and in cafes.
- A privacy screen filter is available on request.

## If a device is lost or taken

Report it within one hour in `#it-incidents` or to `security@omnicorp.example`, or call the regional Service Desk number. If a border official demands a device or password, do not argue; hand it over if legally required, then report it to Security as soon as it is safe.

## Roaming and connectivity

Work-related roaming charges on a personal phone are reimbursed in Concur with the bill. Consider a local eSIM for long trips. Use your phone as a hotspot rather than an untrusted public network.

## Time zones and support

Use the Service Desk phone number for the region you are in: Americas +1 512 555 0142, EMEA +353 1 555 0142, APAC +65 6555 0142. P1 incidents are handled around the clock.

## After you return

Return loaner devices to the IT desk or ship them in the prepaid box within 5 business days. Change your password if any device was lost, searched or unattended during the trip.
