# Omni Corp Remote Work IT Support and Stipends

Document owner: Service Desk with Workplace. Last reviewed: 2026-08-30.

## Equipment for working from home

- **Home office stipend:** one-time 500 USD (or local equivalent) for desk, chair, monitor or lighting, available after your first 90 days. Claim it in Concur under category `Home Office` with receipts.
- **Internet and phone stipend:** 60 USD per month for staff approved as fully remote. It is paid automatically with payroll after your manager flags you as fully remote in Workday.
- IT provides the laptop, dock, headset and, on request, one external monitor. Furniture is not provided by IT.

Peripherals bought with the stipend are yours to keep unless you return IT-supplied items when you leave.

## Network at home

- Use a password-protected home router with WPA2 or WPA3. Change the router admin password from the default.
- Connect to the VPN when you need internal systems. You do not need it for Slack, Zoom, Teams or public sites.
- Do not let family members use your company laptop.

## Home setup problems

| Problem | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Video calls choppy | Use Ethernet through your dock, close cloud-sync apps, and ask others to pause large downloads |
| VPN connects but internal sites fail | Disconnect, reconnect, and confirm the status says Connected. If it still fails, open a ticket under `Network` with a screenshot |
| Second monitor not detected | Reseat the dock cable, restart the laptop, and update from Self-Service or Company Portal |
| Headset not working | Select it as input and output in Zoom/Teams settings, then test with the audio setting |

## Shipping IT equipment

Equipment ships to your home address in the HR system. Update your address in Workday at least five business days before you request hardware. Shipments need a signature.

## Working abroad

You may not work from another country without approval from your manager and People Operations because of tax, legal and security rules. IT will not enable VPN or devices outside your approved country. See the Travel IT Guide for short trips.

## Office hours for remote staff

The remote IT clinic is a Zoom session every Wednesday at 12:00 Central Time. Bring a laptop issue and an agent will troubleshoot with you.
