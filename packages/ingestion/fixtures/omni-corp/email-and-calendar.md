# Omni Corp Email and Calendar (Microsoft 365)

Document owner: Collaboration Services. Last reviewed: 2026-08-18.

Omni Corp email, calendar, Teams and OneDrive run on Microsoft 365. Your address is `firstname.lastname@omnicorp.example`.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Mailbox size | 100 GB, plus an online archive |
| Maximum message size | 25 MB including attachments. Use a OneDrive link for larger files |
| Recipients per message | 500 |
| Deleted items | Recoverable for 30 days from Deleted Items, then 14 more days via the recover-deleted-items tool |

Automatic forwarding to external addresses is disabled. Do not forward company mail to a personal mailbox.

## Shared mailboxes and distribution lists

- A shared mailbox (for example `billing@omnicorp.example`) is requested by a manager through the catalog under `Collaboration`. Access is granted to named people, not shared passwords.
- Distribution lists are owned by a team lead who can add and remove members in Outlook. A new list takes one business day.
- External-looking distribution lists such as customer announcements are created by Marketing Operations.

## Out of office

Set an automatic reply in Outlook for any absence of a day or more, with the date you return and a colleague to contact. Replies to external senders are allowed.

## Calendar etiquette and rooms

- Book meeting rooms in Outlook using the room list for each office. Rooms are released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes.
- Mark private events as **Private**. Free/busy is visible to all employees.
- Use Teams for internal meetings and Zoom for customer meetings. See the Collaboration Tools guide.

## Mail problems

- **Outlook says "Disconnected" or "Trying to connect":** check your network, then restart Outlook. On Windows hold Ctrl while launching Outlook to open in safe mode and disable add-ins. If it persists, sign out of the Microsoft 365 account and back in.
- **Mail missing:** check Junk and the Focused/Other tabs, then search all folders. Quarantined mail is listed at the link in the quarantine digest email.
- **Message blocked by DLP:** see the Data Classification guide and open a ticket under `Security` if it was legitimate.
- **Mailbox full:** empty Deleted Items and move old mail to the online archive.

## Delegates

Executive assistants can be delegates. The mailbox owner grants delegate access in Outlook under **File, Account Settings, Delegate access**.
