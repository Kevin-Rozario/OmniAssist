OMNI CORP ENGINEERING ON-CALL RUNBOOK
Owner: Platform Engineering. Last reviewed 2026-09-18.

PURPOSE
Omni Corp production services have 24x7 on-call coverage. This runbook describes how the
rotation works and what the on-call engineer does during a Sev-1 or Sev-2 incident.

ROTATION
- One primary and one secondary engineer per service group, rotating weekly.
- Handoff is Monday 10:00 local time in the Austin or Bengaluru hub that owns the rotation.
- The current on-call list is shown in PagerDuty and pinned in #it-incidents.
- Swaps are agreed in the team channel and recorded in PagerDuty before the shift starts.

PAGING AND RESPONSE
- Acknowledge a page within 5 minutes.
- If the primary has not acknowledged in 10 minutes, the page goes to the secondary.
- If neither acknowledges in 20 minutes, the engineering manager is paged.
- Sev-1: the on-call engineer becomes or appoints the incident commander and opens an
  incident channel named inc-YYYYMMDD-short-name.
- Sev-2: acknowledge within 30 minutes and start work during business hours of the owning team.

FIRST 15 MINUTES OF A SEV-1
1. Acknowledge the page and confirm the impact (who, what, since when).
2. Create the incident channel and post the first update.
3. Assign roles: incident commander, communications lead, scribe.
4. Post updates every 30 minutes in #it-incidents.
5. For customer-visible impact, update https://status.omnicorp.example.
6. Prefer rollback over a forward fix when a recent change is the suspected cause.

COMMUNICATION
- Internal update template: impact, current status, next update time.
- Do not speculate about root cause in customer-facing messages.
- Security is added to the channel for any suspected compromise.

AFTER THE INCIDENT
- Postmortem within 5 business days for Sev-1 and Sev-2.
- Blameless format: timeline, impact, root cause, what went well, what to improve, actions with
  owners and due dates.
- Actions are tracked in the engineering board and reviewed monthly.

COMPENSATION
On-call engineers receive a weekly stipend and time off in lieu for pages between 22:00 and 06:00.
The exact amounts are in the engineering handbook, not this document.

NOT COVERED HERE
Customer support escalation paths, billing disputes and HR matters are handled by other teams.
