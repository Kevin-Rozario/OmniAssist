# Omni Corp Password Policy

Employees must use a unique password of at least 14 characters for Omni Corp accounts. Passwords must not be reused from personal sites.

## Requirements

- Minimum 14 characters
- At least one letter and one number
- MFA required for SSO, VPN, and email
- Password managers provided by IT are allowed and recommended

## Reset process

Use the self-service portal at https://id.omnicorp.example/reset. Admins must not reset passwords over chat or email.

## Exceptions

Contractors receive 90-day accounts. Extensions require manager approval recorded in the IT ticket.
