# Omni Corp Collaboration Tools: Slack, Teams and Zoom

Document owner: Collaboration Services. Last reviewed: 2026-09-08.

## Which tool for what

| Need | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Quick team questions, announcements, incident coordination | Slack |
| Internal meetings, screen sharing with employees, calls | Microsoft Teams |
| Meetings with customers, partners and candidates | Zoom |
| Large internal events (over 300 people) | Teams live events, booked through Collaboration Services |

Do not use unapproved tools such as WhatsApp or Telegram for company business.

## Slack rules

- Use channels, not direct messages, for work that others may need later.
- Channel naming: `team-`, `proj-`, `help-` and `inc-` prefixes. Incident channels are created by Security as `inc-YYYYMMDD-short-name`.
- Do not post passwords, MFA codes, customer data or Restricted information. Slack messages are retained for 2 years.
- Guest access for external collaborators is requested through the catalog and expires after 90 days unless renewed.

## Zoom

- Customer meetings use your company Zoom account. Sign in with SSO at `omnicorp.zoom.us`.
- Cloud recordings are kept for 180 days and are shared with a passcode. Ask for consent before recording.
- Waiting rooms are on by default. Do not turn them off for meetings with external guests.

## Teams

- Meeting recordings are saved to the organiser's OneDrive and expire after 60 days.
- Use channel meetings for team stand-ups so notes stay with the team.

## Joining problems

- **No audio or camera in Zoom/Teams on Mac:** open System Settings, Privacy and Security, then allow Microphone and Camera for the app. Quit and reopen the app.
- **Echo or feedback:** use a headset. Mute when not speaking.
- **Teams is slow or stuck on a loading screen:** quit Teams, clear the cache folder, and restart. On Windows the cache is under `%appdata%\Microsoft\Teams` for classic Teams or under the package folder for new Teams. Reinstalling from Company Portal also fixes this.
- **Zoom shows "Your connection is unstable":** run a speed test, move closer to the access point, or switch to Ethernet. Avoid joining via VPN since Zoom does not need it with split-tunnel.

## Recording and retention

Meetings with customers must follow the customer contract about recording. When in doubt, do not record, and take written notes instead.
