# Omni Corp Hardware Lifecycle, Repairs and Lost Devices

Document owner: Endpoint Engineering. Last reviewed: 2026-08-12.

## Refresh cycle

Company laptops are replaced every 36 months. You receive an email 60 days before your device is eligible, with a link to choose between the standard models for your role. Phones and tablets are not company-issued except loaner phones for travel.

An earlier replacement is approved only when the device cannot be repaired or no longer meets security requirements.

## Repairs

1. Open a ticket under `Hardware` and describe the fault. Say whether the device powers on.
2. Back up nothing manually: files in OneDrive are already safe. Do not keep important files only on the laptop.
3. For a cracked screen, battery swelling, liquid damage or a failed keyboard, bring the device to the IT desk or ship it with the prepaid label the Service Desk emails you.
4. A loaner laptop is issued the same day at the walk-up desks, or shipped within two business days for remote staff. Repairs normally take three to seven business days.

Accidental damage is repaired at no cost to you the first time. Repeated avoidable damage may be reviewed with your manager.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device **within one hour** of noticing:

1. Message `#it-incidents` or email `security@omnicorp.example`. For a stolen device, also call the regional Service Desk number.
2. Security locks the device and revokes sessions and tokens. Managed laptops are encrypted, and can be remotely wiped from Jamf or Intune.
3. File a police report if the device was stolen and add the report number to the ticket.
4. A replacement is issued after the ticket is verified.

Reporting quickly matters more than certainty. You will not be penalised for reporting a device that is later found.

## Returning equipment

When you leave, or when a refreshed device arrives, return the old device within 10 business days using the prepaid shipping box. Devices are wiped and either reused or recycled by a certified vendor. Do not wipe or reset the device yourself before it is returned.

## Disposal

Never throw away or give away company equipment, drives or media. Contact the Service Desk for secure disposal.
